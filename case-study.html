Case 1 — AI-assisted rule authoring in a regulated platform

Governed, real-time patient-engagement interventions

BrightInsight (regulated digital health) · Principal PM
Problem. Engagement logic for patient journeys was hard to define, slow to change, and difficult to audit — a poor fit for a platform serving multiple regulated healthcare product lines that must prove what fired, for whom, and why.
Approach. Led an AI-assisted, MCP-based rule-authoring workflow: an agent/tool architecture with human-in-the-loop validation, a configurable “rules-as-code” model, and a vectorization pipeline with semantic search for governed content discovery and recommendation.
Outcome.
10,000+patients served by real-time AI-assisted interventions
100%audit traceability for regulated journeys
0→1set the pattern for governed AI features on the platform
Shows: 0→1 in regulated AI, agentic architecture with guardrails, and product + technical depth working together.
